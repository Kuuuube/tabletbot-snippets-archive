**Enable pressure support for Windows**

- Install [VMulti Driver](https://github.com/X9VoiD/vmulti-bin/releases/latest) (NOT VMultiMode)
- Install and follow instructions from [WindowsInk's wiki](https://github.com/Kuuuube/VoiDPlugins/wiki/WindowsInk)
- Configure your application to use Windows Ink API. See [here](https://opentabletdriver.net/Wiki/FAQ/WindowsAppSpecific#art-programs) for app-specific configuration instructions.

After that, you should be good to go!
