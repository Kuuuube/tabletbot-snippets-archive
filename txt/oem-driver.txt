**Running OTD along side OEM drivers**

Having OEM drivers (Wacom, Huion, XP-Pen, etc.) installed while using OpenTabletDriver is unsupported as they will take precedence over OpenTabletDriver.
At best the settings you have in OpenTabletDriver will not apply, at worst you will experience double/teleporting cursor.
We recommend uninstalling any OEM drivers you have installed and only use OpenTabletDriver.
