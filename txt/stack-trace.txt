**How to find stack traces on Windows**

- Open Event Viewer (you can find it by pressing the windows key and searching `Event Viewer`)
- Expand `Windows Logs` on the left menu
- Select `Application`
- Find an error with `.NET Runtime` in the `Source` column and check that the error is from OTD
- Copy the contents of the box where it says "Application: OpenTabletDriver..."
- Send what you have copied here
