**If you are experiencing crashing with OpenTabletDriver**

- Make a new folder and move the OpenTabletDriver executables there
- Make sure that there is __no__ `Configurations` folder if you are using any version higher than 0.5.3.3.
- Delete or move `%localappdata%\\OpenTabletDriver\\settings.json`
- Delete or move `%localappdata%\\OpenTabletDriver\\Plugins`
