**Get Tablet Specs**

- Open the debugger in OpenTabletDriver from the top menu. `Tablets` > `Tablet Debugger`.
- Find the highest position values possible for your tablet. This is typically in the bottom right corner.
  If you are having trouble with this, try moving very slowly towards the end of the tablet's area.
  You may also do each axis one at a time. For example, moving to the middle of the right side for X and the middle of the bottom for Y.
- Find the highest pressure value possible. Press down on the pen until the pressure value stops increasing.
